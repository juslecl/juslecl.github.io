About

I'm a PhD candidate in the Biostatistics and Epidemiology program at the Life Science Graduate School Zürich, co-organized by UZH and ETH Zürich. I work within the Center of Experimental Rheumatology at USZ, supervised by Asst. Prof. Dr. Christof Seiler, and the Department of Molecular Life Sciences at UZH, supervised by Prof. Dr. Mark Robinson. My PhD project is the RAI Platform — Reliable AI for Biomedicine. Before my PhD, I completed an MSc in Statistics at ETH Zürich (grade 5.47/6), with a thesis on linear regression with known error distribution supervised by Prof. Dr. Fadoua Balabdaoui. I also hold a BSc in Applied Mathematics (with Honors) from Paris I Panthéon-Sorbonne and a BA in Social Sciences (Cum Laude, double major in Sociology and Economics) from Sciences Po Paris, plus an Erasmus exchange year at the Universität Wien.
